    Learning Center

    RSI (Relative Strength Index): Measures the speed and change of price movements. RSI > 70 is overbought (sell signal), RSI < 30 is oversold (buy signal).

    MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): A trend-following momentum indicator. When the MACD line crosses above the signal line, it's a bullish (buy) signal.

    Sharpe Ratio: A measure of risk-adjusted return. Higher is better. A ratio > 1 is good, > 2 is very good.

    Max Drawdown: The maximum observed loss from a peak to a trough of a portfolio. Lower is better.
